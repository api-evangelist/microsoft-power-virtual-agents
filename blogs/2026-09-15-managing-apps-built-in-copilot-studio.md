Last week we announced app building in Copilot Studio and Copilot Cowork . Makers can now describe business outcomes and produce full-stack apps with built-in source control, deployment stages, and version isolation—all on Microsoft-hosted infrastructure that requires no infrastructure provisioning, hosting configuration, or deployment pipeline. With those capabilities built in, your administrative scope is narrower and more familiar.
